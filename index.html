WHAT IS PEPPOL (INVOICENOW) E-INVOICING?

InvoiceNow is a nationwide e-invoicing network that enables companies of all sizes to process invoices in a more efficient manner, reducing business costs and shortening payment cycles. InvoiceNow operates on the open standard Peppol framework, which directly transmits e-invoices in a standard digital format across different finance systems.

InvoiceNow is Singapore’s nationwide e-invoicing framework, built on the Peppol network, enabling businesses to send and receive invoices digitally between accounting systems. It eliminates manual data entry, reduces errors, and improves payment efficiency.

On April 15, 2024, the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) announced a phased adoption of InvoiceNow for GST-registered businesses. This requires businesses to transmit invoice data to IRAS via the InvoiceNow network using InvoiceNow-Ready Solutions.

  • November 1, 2025 – Newly incorporated companies that voluntarily register for GST must use InvoiceNow.
  • April 1, 2026 – All new voluntary GST-registrants must comply.
  • May 1, 2025 – Soft launch for early adopters (existing GST-registered businesses or businesses applying for GST registration after May 1, 2025).
  • Future phases will extend the requirement to all GST-registered businesses.
